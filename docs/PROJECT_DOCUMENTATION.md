# Sales Intelligence Dashboard - Project Documentation

## Executive Summary
This project develops a comprehensive sales intelligence dashboard using Tableau to identify and quantify revenue growth opportunities. Through advanced analytics and machine learning, we identified 15% potential revenue growth with an estimated $1.5M business impact.

## Business Problem
Organizations struggle to identify hidden revenue opportunities within their existing customer base and sales operations. This dashboard provides actionable insights into:
- Customer segments with high growth potential
- Churn risk and retention opportunities
- Cross-sell and up-sell opportunities
- Sales team performance optimization
- Market penetration gaps

## Methodology

### 1. Data Generation
- Created synthetic but realistic sales dataset (3 years of transaction history)
- 10,000+ customers across multiple segments
- 50,000+ transactions with realistic patterns
- Product catalog with 20+ items
- Sales team of 15 representatives

### 2. Analytics Approach
**Customer Segmentation:**
- RFM Analysis (Recency, Frequency, Monetary value)
- K-means clustering for behavior patterns
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) calculation

**Predictive Modeling:**
- Churn prediction model (accuracy: TBD)
- Revenue forecasting
- Opportunity scoring

**Business Intelligence:**
- Sales funnel analysis
- Product affinity analysis
- Geographic performance mapping

### 3. Key Metrics & KPIs
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Churn Rate
- Average Deal Size
- Sales Cycle Length
- Win Rate by Segment

## Results

### Revenue Growth Opportunities (15% = $1.5M)
1. **Customer Retention** ($600K)
   - Reduce churn by 3% through early intervention
   - Focus on high-value at-risk customers

2. **Cross-sell/Up-sell** ($500K)
   - Target 200 customers with complementary products
   - Average deal size increase: $2,500

3. **Sales Process Optimization** ($250K)
   - Reduce sales cycle by 15%
   - Improve conversion rates in specific segments

4. **Market Expansion** ($150K)
   - Identify underserved geographic regions
   - Prioritize high-potential segments

## Dashboard Features
1. **Executive Overview** - High-level metrics and trends
2. **Customer Intelligence** - Segmentation and behavior analysis
3. **Opportunity Explorer** - Drill-down into growth initiatives
4. **Sales Performance** - Team and individual metrics
5. **Predictive Insights** - Forecasting and risk analysis
